Every *worldwide* management-consulting figure is either an aggregator (USD 357.85B / 491.68B / 1,063.77B) or a specialist analyst figure that could not be read from its own page (Source Global Research USD 275bn, HTTP 403, snippet only). **No government statistical agency publishes a global total for either market** — Census is US-only, Eurostat is EU-only and publishes value added rather than turnover for NACE M.
- **Accenture's 10-K contains no total-addressable-market statement.** Grep of the full filing text for "addressable market" and "market for our services" returned zero hits; the only market-scale language is qualitative ("The markets in which we offer our solutions and services are highly competitive"). Deloitte, EY and KPMG likewise publish revenue but no TAM.
- **Deloitte and KPMG publish segment *growth rates* but no absolute segment revenues**, so their consulting-only revenue cannot be extracted. EY, PwC and Accenture do publish absolute segment figures.
- **IBISWorld and Statista yielded nothing** (405 / redirect loop). No Kennedy/ALM Intelligence primary source was reachable — the ALM figure exists only as a second-hand, undated citation on Consultancy.uk.

# (3) DEFINITION DRIFT — how the two markets overlap and double-count

**a) Management consulting is nested inside professional services by every source that addresses the relationship.**
- Consultancy.eu: *"the global consulting sector is one of the largest and most mature markets within the professional services industry"* (https://www.consultancy.eu/consulting-industry/global, HTTP 200).
- Precedence Research segments its **professional services** market by service type as *"Management Consulting Services, Strategy Consulting Services, Cloud Professional Services, Engineering Services"* — i.e. its Market-A number already contains Market B (https://www.precedenceresearch.com/professional-services-market, HTTP 200).
- The Business Research Company's **professional services** segments include *"Design, Research, Promotional And Consulting Services, Legal Services, Accounting Services, All Other Professional, Scientific, Technical Services"* — same nesting (HTTP 200).
- In official statistics the nesting is exact and hierarchical: NAICS 54161 (Management Consulting) ⊂ NAICS 5416 (Management, Scientific and Technical Consulting) ⊂ NAICS 54 (Professional, Scientific and Technical Services). **Adding a management-consulting figure to a professional-services figure double-counts.**

**b) The sources themselves say the estimates are not reconcilable.**
- Consultancy.org / Consultancy.uk: *"The 'gap' between the estimates follows from, among others, different scoping of markets, varying definitions applied for consulting services, differences in regions/markets considered and the nature of calculation models used."* Also: *"the large variety of services (industries, functional areas, market/region focus) that span the consulting industry imply that there is no clear-cut consensus on how the market should be defined."* (both HTTP 200).

**c) Observed disagreement, reported without reconciliation (per rule 4):**
- **Global professional services 2025:** TBRC/Research and Markets USD 6,370.29B vs Precedence USD 1.20T — a **5.3× spread on the same year**. Technavio publishes only an increment (USD 1,944.3B over 2025-2030) and no base, so it cannot be placed on either scale.
- **Global management consulting 2025:** Source Global Research USD 275B (snippet, LOW) vs Mordor USD 357.85B vs Fortune Business Insights USD 491.68B vs TBRC USD 1,063.77B — a **3.9× spread**.
- **Same analyst, two incompatible numbers:** Source Global Research is cited at **USD 120B** for "the top segment of management consultancy" (Consultancy.uk), at **USD 275B** for the global consulting market in 2025 (snippet), and at **>USD 400B for *technology consulting alone* in 2026** (ITPro, HTTP 200). The tech-consulting-only figure exceeds the all-consulting figure, so the three are on three different definitions.
- **Official vs private on the same geography:** US Census NAICS 54161 US management-consulting revenue was **USD 307,310M in 2022** (employer firms). Source Global Research via Consultancy.uk put **total US management consulting spend at USD 58.7B (~2016)** — ~5× smaller. Census measures firm *revenue* in an industry classification; Source measures client *fee spend* on a narrower service definition.

**d) Boundary rules that move the number, quoted:**
- Mordor is the only source with an explicit exclusion list: excludes *"IT implementation-only outsourcing, software licensing revenue, and pure staffing or recruitment services"* unless sold inside a consulting engagement — this is precisely the boundary TBRC crosses when it reaches USD 1,063.77B by defining the market as *"advising **and implementing** services"* (emphasis on the implementation clause, quoted verbatim above).
- Accenture's 10-K draws its own line: *"Consulting revenues, which include strategy, management and technology consulting and technology integration consulting, reflect a finite, distinct project or set of projects with a defined outcome"* — so **Accenture's USD 35.11B "Consulting" includes technology integration**, which Mordor would exclude and TBRC would include.
- US Census excludes one industry from NAICS 54 outright: *"(9) Excludes NAICS 54112 (Offices of notaries)."* (QSS) and *"Excludes NAICS 54112 (Offices of Notaries)."* (SAS footnote 6).
- **Employer vs nonemployer changes the US professional-services total by USD 230,259M in 2022 alone** (USD 2,841,227M incl. nonemployers vs USD 2,610,968M employer-only, same source, same year). The QSS quarterly series is employer-firms-only; the SAS annual Table 1 is not. Mixing them understates or overstates by ~8%.
- Eurostat's EUR 779.3B for NACE Section M is **value added, not turnover** — it is not on the same basis as any revenue figure in this dossier and must not be compared to one.
- Big Four "global revenue" figures (Deloitte USD 70.5B, PwC USD 56.9B, EY USD 53.2B, KPMG USD 39.8B) are **aggregations of legally separate member firms across differing fiscal year-ends** (31 May, 30 June, 30 June, 30 Sept respectively) — unaudited network aggregates, unlike Accenture's SEC-filed consolidated USD 69.67B.
