## (3) DEFINITION DRIFT

**A absorbs B (Statista).** Statista's IT Services market is composed of "IT Consulting & Implementation, Business Process Outsourcing, IT Outsourcing, and Other IT Services (covering system integration, software support, and IT training)." Its BPO page confirms: within the IT Services hierarchy, "Business Process Outsourcing operates as a distinct segment alongside IT Consulting & Implementation, IT Outsourcing… and other IT services." So **Statista's USD 1.57 tn (2026) already contains its USD 434.99 bn (2026) of BPO** — adding Statista's A and B double-counts ~28% of A.

**A absorbs IaaS, then stops (Gartner).** In the April 2026 release Gartner's IT Services is "IT services, including application implementation and managed services, infrastructure implementation and managed services and IaaS" — i.e. cloud infrastructure consumption sits *inside* the services number. By the July 2026 release Gartner split that same block into "Services \| 1,492 \| 3.8 \| 1,570 \| 5.3" and "Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) \| 222 \| 25.3 \| 287 \| 29.3". Any model anchored on Gartner "IT services = 1.87 T" is anchored on a definition Gartner retired three months later. Gartner's public tables never break out BPO; BPO is presumably inside "IT Services"/"Services" but the press releases never say so.

**A and B are siblings, not nested (NASSCOM, ISG).** NASSCOM reports "IT Services $137.1 Bn" and "BPM $54.6 Bn" as separate lines of one industry total, alongside ER&D, software products and hardware. ISG likewise treats them as sibling service lines: "Service Lines: ITO up 2%; Engineering up 35%; BPO down 14%." Under these two frames A and B do not double-count each other — but they are also not summable with Gartner's or Statista's A.

**Stock vs flow.** ISG's numbers are labelled on every slide "ACV = Annual Contract Value" and measure newly awarded contracts, not revenue delivered. ISG's entire combined market — "Reached nearly $40B for first time ever" (1Q26) — is ~2% of Gartner's quarterly IT-services spend. The two must never be placed on the same axis.

**Cross-border vs total.** The World Bank/IMF figure counts only exports: "Information and communication technology service exports include computer and communications services (telecommunications and postal and courier services) and information services (computer data and news-related service transactions)." USD 1.279 tn (2024) is trade flow, and its inclusion of telecom/postal/courier makes it broader than IT services on one axis while being narrower (exports only) on another.

**Vendors don't use either label.** No major provider reports on an IT-services-vs-BPO axis, so a bottom-up build cannot be split between A and B:
- Accenture: "We deliver two types of work: Consulting and Managed Services" — Consulting $35.1 B / Managed Services $34.6 B.
- Genpact: "Data-Tech-AI services" $2,442.4 M and "Digital Operations services" $2,637.5 M — the word "BPO" does not appear as a reporting label anywhere in its FY2025 10-K.
- Concentrix: "a global technology and services leader that powers exceptional brand experiences and digital operations… including customer experience ("CX") process optimization, technology innovation and design engineering, front- and back-office automation, analytics and business transformation services".

**Overlap with "professional services."** The US statistical system places IT services *inside* professional services: NAICS 5415 (Computer Systems Design and Related Services) is a child of NAICS 54 (Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services). Census SAS 2022 Table 1 puts NAICS 54 at **2,841,227** ($M, employer and nonemployer firms) against 5415's **648,805** ($M, employer firms) — so US "IT services" is roughly a fifth to a quarter of officially measured US professional services, and sits beside, not inside, NAICS 5416 "Management, scientific, and technical consulting services" (112,125 $M in 1Q2026 NSA). Management consulting is therefore *excluded* from every "IT services" figure above but *included* in most colloquial uses of "professional services" — and BPO/business support (5614) is inside NAICS 56, a different sector entirely from both.
