## Cross-cutting notes for the study team

1. **TaskUs is the most important nuance in this dataset.** A definitive-agreement take-private at $16.50/share was announced 2025-05-09, but a major shareholder (Think Investments) publicly opposed it and ISS recommended rejection (~Sept 2025). TaskUs was still trading publicly and reporting quarterly earnings as of Q2 2026 (~Aug 6, 2026) and its market cap ($740.83M) implies a share price (~$8) far below the $16.50 deal price — strongly suggesting the deal did not close as originally structured. This session could not obtain a definitive closing/termination announcement (both the Nasdaq press-release fetch and a BusinessWire fetch timed out). **Recommend another lane attempt a direct SEC EDGAR pull on TaskUs 8-K/DEFM14A filings to resolve current status.**
2. **Figma is a clean "IPO instead of exit" case study**: Adobe's $20B acquisition attempt (2022) collapsed under EU/UK regulatory pressure in Dec 2023 (Adobe paid a $1B breakup fee), and Figma subsequently IPO'd in July 2025 at a $56.3B day-one valuation — which has since fallen to ~$14.3B (Aug 2026).
3. **Big consulting incumbents (Big Four + MBB) show a visible AI-acquisition gap** in this dataset relative to AlixPartners, Capgemini, Teleperformance and Cognizant. Multiple direct searches for Deloitte/EY/KPMG/PwC/McKinsey/Bain/BCG AI-startup acquisitions returned nothing; this may reflect actual scarcity, a search-tool blind spot, or firms doing AI capability-building via internal build/partnership (e.g., KPMG–Google Cloud $100M partnership, IBM–OpenAI Aug 2026 partnership) rather than M&A.
4. **Market-cap snapshot is uniformly dated** to stockanalysis.com's 2026-08-19 4:00pm EDT close across the small/mid-cap IT-services names (EPAM, Globant, Endava, Genpact, Concentrix, Grid Dynamics, Innodata, Upwork, Fiverr, ZipRecruiter, Figma) — all show significant year-over-year declines (−40% to −78%), a pattern worth flagging to whichever lane is doing valuation/multiple analysis.
5. **Grammarly's 2025 rebrand to "Superhuman Platform"** following the Coda and Superhuman acquisitions is itself a naming/identity-change signal that may be relevant to the study's consolidation narrative, independent of the deal facts themselves.
