Reported settled ~May 2026 (ads removed from NY/SF, no financial terms disclosed; Green: "reached a settlement pretty quick"). — https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/03/this-is-fine-creator-says-ai-startup-stole-his-art/ and https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/31/this-is-fine-artist-kc-green-reaches-agreement-with-ai-startup-artisan/ (both 200, loaded, REPUTABLE_MEDIA, HIGH)
