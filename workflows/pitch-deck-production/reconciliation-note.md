# Reconciliation note — one discrepancy, not guessed away

Stage 2 compared the draft deck against the two documents it drew on: the
published White Paper and the Investor Room copy on talomnia.com. The comparison
found one substantive discrepancy, and the way it was handled is the point of
this note.

## The discrepancy

The figure **“25–50 organizations”** appeared in both sources with two different
populations behind it:

| Source | What the number counted |
|---|---|
| White Paper | paying organizations |
| Investor Room | design partners **and** clients |

These are not the same set, so the two documents were not saying the same thing
with the same number.

## The decision

The workflow did **not** choose a meaning. Picking one reading would have made
the deck internally consistent and externally unsupported — the figure would have
carried an authority no source gave it. Instead **the number was omitted from the
Pitch Deck**, and the discrepancy was recorded here.

The same rule was applied to the round: a pre-seed **hypothesis** is not
presented as an announced round, and not as a measured capital requirement.

Traction and commercial metrics are absent from the deck because there are no
commercial cases. That absence is the honest state of a pre-commercial company,
and it is stated rather than filled.

## Why this is in the public trace

An investor reading the deck should be able to see not only what it claims, but
what it declined to claim and why. A reconciliation that quietly resolves a
conflict leaves no trace; one that omits the figure and says so leaves this file.

The upstream fix — making the two sources agree on what they are counting — is a
content question for the White Paper and the Investor Room, and is not resolved
by this workflow.
